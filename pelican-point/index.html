The Cottage on Pelican Point

Meredith Calloway

At forty-six, she lost the life she knew — and found the one she was meant to live, in a small town by the sea.

Her husband's secret ended twenty-one years of marriage. Her mother's old cottage on Pelican Point was supposed to be a place to fall apart. It became the place she began again — and she didn't rebuild it alone.
