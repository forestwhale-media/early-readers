The Amish Marriage Agreement

Louisa Bennett

A widowed farmer, a woman the community overlooked, and a practical marriage neither expects to become more.

When widower Jacob proposes a sensible arrangement for the sake of his three boys, Miriam accepts — and finds that everyday kindness can grow into something neither of them dared hope for. An uplifting Amish romance of faith, family, and second chances.
